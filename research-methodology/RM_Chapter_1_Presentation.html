Who Should Conduct the Research?

Internal Researcher

Knowledge: strong organisational familiarity

Access: often easier

Implementation: can remain involved

Watch: politics, fewer fresh ideas, perceived bias

OR

External Researcher

Perspective: fresh / more independent

Expertise: specialised, broader experience

Impartiality: useful for sensitive issues

Watch: higher cost, less familiarity

The choice depends on what the research problem requires — not on which type is always “better.”

You Decide: Internal or External?

A

Routine operational problem

Delivery delays have increased. The organisation wants a quick investigation using internal records and staff knowledge.

INTERNAL RESEARCHER
Internal staff already have organisational knowledge, access to records and can investigate a routine operational issue quickly.

Can Good Research Be Unethical?

You collect excellent data and obtain a useful answer…

but participants were pressured or misled.

Is it good research?

Voluntary participation & informed consent

Understand the research; no improper pressure.

Privacy, anonymity & confidentiality

Protect personal information and responses.

Avoid harm

Avoid unnecessary physical, psychological, social or professional harm.

Honesty & integrity

No fabricated data, manipulated results or deliberate misrepresentation.

Ethics applies throughout the research process.
