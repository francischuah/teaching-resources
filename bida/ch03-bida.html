Quick critique
Good chart or bad chart?
Look at the actual visual first. Click each card after students identify the problem.
CHAPTER 3 · DATA VISUALISATION & EXPLORATION
Too many pie slices
Many similar angles are difficult to compare. A sorted bar chart is usually clearer.
Unnecessary 3D
Perspective changes apparent height and makes accurate comparison harder.
Too many colours
If colour has no meaning, every bar competes for attention instead of guiding it.
Heavy gridlines
The supporting lines become more prominent than the data itself.
Critique the visual by asking: what helps, and what competes with the message?17 / 26
