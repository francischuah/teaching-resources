PART 1 · SUMMARISING DATA

Comparing variability fairly

A smaller standard deviation alone may reflect a smaller scale.
BIDA
DESCRIPTIVE STATISTICS

Coefficient of variation

CV = standard deviation ÷ mean

Express the result as a percentage. CV compares spread relative to the average.

=STDEV.S(range)/AVERAGE(range)

Use with care

Use variables with a meaningful zero and a positive mean away from zero.

A lower CV indicates less relative variability. It does not automatically mean a better business result.

Reveal explanation
Avoid CV for Celsius temperatures or a mean near zero. For skewed data, also inspect the median, IQR and shape.

DESCRIPTIVE STATISTICS

Use the histogram

Inspect symmetry, tails, clusters and gaps. A long right tail indicates positive skewness.

Use the boxplot

Compare median, central spread and flagged observations.

Use a z-score for relative distance

z = (value − mean) ÷ SD

z = +2 means two standard deviations above the mean.

|z| > 3 can flag candidates, but skewness and extreme values can affect this screening method.
